# V0.11 — Market, Storage & Orders

This internal iteration connects fixed Farm Market and fixed Storage into the economy loop.

Flow:
Harvest -> Storage -> Production -> Storage -> Market/Orders -> Coins/XP/Gems

Market is fixed like the Water Pump and Mill. Storage is fixed and does not consume a movable decoration slot.

Prices use data-driven min/base/max bands. Final balance must be playtested before commercial release.
